Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 19, 2025

Introduction

Sav-N-Stack ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the App.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We collect the following personal information that you voluntarily provide to us:

  • Financial Information: Savings goals, target amounts, current savings amounts, and funding transactions
  • Journal Entries: Personal notes, medication reminders, supplement information, and appointment alerts
  • Biometric Data: Fingerprint or facial recognition data (stored locally on your device only for authentication purposes)

Automatically Collected Information

When you use the App, we may automatically collect:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features used, time spent in the app, interaction patterns
  • Log Data: Error logs, crash reports, and performance data

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide Services: Enable you to track savings goals, manage financial targets, and use journal features
  • Authentication: Secure your account using biometric authentication
  • Improve the App: Analyze usage patterns to enhance features and user experience
  • Send Notifications: Deliver alerts for medication reminders, appointments, and savings milestones
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
🔒 Local Storage Only

All your data is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit, store, or process your personal information on external servers or cloud services. This means:

  • Your savings goals, journal entries, and all app data remain on your device
  • No data is sent to external servers or third parties
  • Your information is never shared with cloud storage providers
  • You have complete control over your data

Data Storage and Security

Security Measures

We implement security measures to protect your information:

  • Local Encryption: Data stored on your device is protected by your device's security features
  • Biometric Authentication: Fingerprint and facial recognition for secure app access (processed locally on your device)
  • No Network Transmission: Your personal data never leaves your device

Data Retention

Your data remains on your device until you choose to delete it. You can delete individual entries or uninstall the app to remove all data from your device.

Data Backup

Since all data is stored locally:

  • If you uninstall the app, all data will be permanently deleted
  • We recommend using your device's backup features (iCloud, Google Drive) if you want to preserve your data
  • We do not provide cloud backup services

Bank Account Integration

Current Status: The App currently uses mock bank integration for demonstration purposes. Real bank account integration is planned for future releases.

Future Integration: When real bank integration is implemented, we will:

  • Use secure, industry-standard financial data aggregation services
  • Only access read-only information with your explicit consent
  • Never store your bank login credentials
  • Update this privacy policy to reflect the specific service provider used

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

Expo Notifications

  • Purpose: Push notifications for reminders and alerts
  • Data Shared: Device tokens, notification preferences (no personal data)
  • Privacy Policy: https://expo.dev/privacy

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

Since all data is stored locally on your device, we do not have access to your personal information and cannot share it. We may only share anonymized, non-personal information in the following circumstances:

  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • Protection of Rights: To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property, and that of our users

Your Privacy Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

Access and Portability

  • All your data is accessible directly within the App
  • You can view and export your savings goals and journal entries at any time

Correction

  • Update or correct information directly through the App

Deletion

  • Delete individual entries within the App
  • Uninstall the App to remove all data from your device

Opt-Out

  • Disable push notifications in your device settings
  • Disable biometric authentication in the App settings

Data Minimization

  • We only collect data necessary to provide App functionality
  • You can choose not to use optional features (e.g., journal entries)

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately at cohibas4u@protonmail.com.

International Data Transfers

Since all data is stored locally on your device, there are no international data transfers. Your information remains on your device regardless of your location.

California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • Right to know what personal information is collected (all data is stored locally on your device)
  • Right to know if personal information is sold or disclosed (we do not sell or disclose your information)
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell your information)
  • Right to request deletion of personal information (delete data within the App or uninstall)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights

To exercise these rights or for questions, contact us at cohibas4u@protonmail.com.

European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right to access your personal data (accessible within the App)
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data (edit within the App)
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") (delete within the App or uninstall)
  • Right to restrict processing (all processing is local on your device)
  • Right to data portability (export features available in the App)
  • Right to object to processing (you control all data on your device)
  • Right to withdraw consent (uninstall the App)

To exercise these rights or for questions, contact us at cohibas4u@protonmail.com.
